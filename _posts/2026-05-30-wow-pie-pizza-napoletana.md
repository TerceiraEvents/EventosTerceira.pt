*Vem, come, diverte-te!* — Come, eat, have fun. That's the invitation from **WOW PIE**, a pizza project bringing authentic Italian artisan Neapolitan pizza to Terceira.

WOW PIE follows the guidelines of the **Associazione Verace Pizza Napoletana** (AVPN), the body that defines what true Neapolitan pizza is, combining that strict tradition with the rich terroir of the island.

### What goes into the dough

The whole point is simplicity done properly. The dough uses **only flour, water, salt and yeast** — zero sugar, no chemicals, no preservatives. Thanks to a **24-hour natural fermentation**, the starches break down before baking, so the pizza comes out incredibly light and easy to digest.

### Real food, made with integrity

On top of that base go **extra virgin olive oil**, antioxidant-rich tomatoes, and fresh ingredients from the island's green pastures and volcanic soil. As they put it, this is authentic Italian heritage — kissed by the Atlantic. Real food, made with integrity, and signed by pizzaiolo **Syed Bashar**.
