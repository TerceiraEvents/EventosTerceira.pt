Twins is back.

After the [temporary closure announced in late April]({{ '/blog/2026/twins-temporarily-closed/' | relative_url }}), the Angra club reopens this weekend with a two-night relaunch:

- **Saturday, May 23 — Massivedrum**, warm-up by Rodrigo.
- **Sunday, May 24 — Malvada**, warm-up by Vitor. (Sunday is the eve of a public holiday, so it's the second of two big nights, not a one-off.)

Doors at **midnight** both nights. As the flyer puts it: *come early, leave late.*

The Friday/Saturday weekly slot is also coming back — Twins is now listed on the [weekly schedule]({{ '/weekly/' | relative_url }}) for both nights, with the headlining DJ and warm-up rotating week to week. Check [@twinstheclub](https://www.instagram.com/twinstheclub/) on Instagram for each weekend's lineup.
