*O teu paraíso de jogos na Terceira* — "your gaming paradise on Terceira." That's how **Escudo & 20** describes itself, and the doors open at **Rua da Palha 86, in Angra do Heroísmo**, on **Friday, 20 November at 14:00** Azores time.

### What you'll find inside

The club's own summary of the programme: **board games, card games, video games, tabletop and LAN parties** — *tabuleiro, cartas, videojogos, tabletop e LAN parties*. One address for all of it.

### Opening hours

- **Monday:** closed
- **Tuesday–Thursday:** 14:00–23:00
- **Friday & Saturday:** 14:00–02:00
- **Sunday:** 14:00–23:00

Friday and Saturday are the long nights — gaming until 02:00.

### Playing and membership

You can drop in with a **day pass (passe diário)** or become a **monthly member (sócio mensal)**. Events have limited seats, and members reserve theirs through the club app.

### The programme so far

The agenda shows what the club ran before opening the café: October's game nights included a plain *Noite de tabuleiro* (board-game night, Friday 9 October), a **Rock Band 4 jam session** with Chris hosting, in English (Thursday 8 October), and a board-game night with **Dominion and Dutch Blitz**, also hosted in English (Tuesday 6 October). Bilingual nights look to be part of the house style.
