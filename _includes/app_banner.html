{%- comment -%} One-time mobile-app smart banner. Renders an empty
that the script below populates only when the visitor is on iOS or Android AND hasn't previously dismissed the banner (tracked in localStorage under a versioned key). Desktop visitors and dismissers see nothing — the script bails out before any DOM is written, so there's no layout shift. Localization: the visible strings live in the script as a small bag keyed by `page.lang`, mirroring the pattern used by `window.__SITE_I18N` in `_layouts/default.html`. Suppression on form pages: pages whose path contains `/suggest/` (the EN and PT submit-an-event forms) are skipped so the banner doesn't interrupt the submission flow. Versioned storage key: `tev_app_banner_dismissed_v1`. Bump the `_v` suffix to re-show the banner to everyone after a redesign or new app launch. {%- endcomment -%}
