{%- comment -%} EN/PT locale switcher. Each page sets `lang_alt:` in its front matter to the URL of its counterpart in the other language (e.g. an English `/contribute/` sets `lang_alt: /pt/contribute/` and vice versa). When `lang_alt` is missing we fall back to the language root (`/` or `/pt/`), which keeps the toggle functional on pages that don't yet have a translation. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign current_lang = page.lang | default: "en" -%} {%- if current_lang == "pt" -%} {%- assign other_url = page.lang_alt | default: "/" -%} {%- assign other_label = "EN" -%} {%- assign other_full = "English" -%} {%- assign current_label = "PT" -%} {%- else -%} {%- assign other_url = page.lang_alt | default: "/pt/" -%} {%- assign other_label = "PT" -%} {%- assign other_full = "Português" -%} {%- assign current_label = "EN" -%} {%- endif -%}
