# Terceira event audit — 2026-10-10

Checked in `Atlantic/Azores`. Publication lookback: 24 September onward, plus pinned programmes and current institutional schedules. Event window: 10 October 2026 through 8 January 2027, retaining ongoing exhibitions and verified later announcements.

Result: **41 additions and 10 corrected records**, from 605 to 646 special events. Complete-file duplicate, tag, date, and image-host checks apply to the final data. The normalized duplicate validator identifies the Monte Brasil festival presentation as already covered; the festival itself is a distinct addition.

This is a broad public-source and signed-in browser audit, **not exhaustive island-wide coverage**. The library flipbook programme, private groups, stories, image-only reels, and some parish programmes remain incomplete. A source's reachable homepage or healthy ingester is not proof that its entire programme is covered.

## Completion requirements

An exhaustive claim requires the readable BiblioAgenda programme, remaining social image-only/video announcements and group albums, parish committee programmes, and story/highlight coverage with an explicit feed/date boundary for every source. Museu automation also requires unattended access permitted by its operator. The report keeps those gaps open; it does not count blocked, untested, or partially read sources as empty.
